First time installing?

Get started with Primera.

  1. 01

    Download Primera

    Tap “Download Primera” to download the APK to your Android phone.

  2. 02

    Install the app

    Open the downloaded APK and allow installation when prompted.

  3. 03

    Set up Primera

    Open Primera and follow the on-screen instructions to create your account and complete your profile.

  4. 04

    Connect your smartwatch

    Install OHealth, pair your compatible smartwatch, then allow Primera to access supported health data through Health Connect.

Primera is available for compatible Android devices. Smartwatch features require a compatible device, OHealth, and Health Connect.

Make Primera yours

How to use Primera.

  1. 01

    Complete your profile

    Enter the information requested by Primera to set up your account.

  2. 02

    Connect your health data

    View supported information such as steps, sleep, heart rate, and blood oxygen when available from your connected device.

  3. 03

    Complete health check-ins

    Record pregnancy-related information such as symptoms, medication, mood, and daily wellbeing. You can use voice input when available.

  4. 04

    Track your goals

    Set wellness goals and monitor your progress through the app.

  5. 05

    Explore your dashboard

    Review your health information, progress, and pregnancy-related resources in one place.

  6. 06

    Review your health summary

    Use your recorded information and available summary features to help organize your health information for your records or healthcare provider.

Primera supports pregnancy self-monitoring and wellness tracking. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace professional medical care.
